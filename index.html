About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Yale, advised by Dr. David van Dijk. My research develops machine-learning methods for biological data, including representing single-cell gene expression as natural language (Cell2Sentence), modeling cell–cell interactions in spatial transcriptomics with ligand–receptor priors, and vision–language models and agentic systems for interpreting fMRI recordings.

I am interested in interpretable and multimodal models, few-shot evaluation, and workflows that integrate prior biological knowledge. Before graduate school I worked as a software engineer and completed a dual Masters at the University of Michigan in Computer Science and Mathematics.
